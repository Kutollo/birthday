I got you a small gift

I chose it because it represents time… and how some things don’t really disappear, they just pause and continue in different ways.

Every moment is something I wanted you to feel held in, even when I’m not there. Not rushed, not forgotten, not replaced… just gently remembered in the way that stays.

I hope you wear it often. Not just as something pretty, but as a reminder that you are valued, remembered, cared for, loved and genuinely appreciated.

I hope it will never leave your wrist, just like how you never really left my heart 🥹
